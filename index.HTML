Why choose waveRiders

At WaveRiders, we are passionate about helping you ride the waves with confidence and joy.

ⓘCertified, bushy-tailed instructors!

Our nutty instructors are fully certified in squirrel surf safety and bring a tree-load of experience in every lesson. They are not just acorn-catching pros on the waves, they are also friendly, patient and know how to make even the shyest squirrel feel at ease.

ⓘTiny class sizes to notch for nut-struction

We keep our classes small , just a pawful of squirrels at a time. so every student gets plenty of one-on-one attention. This way your instructor can focus on your unique skills and help you scurry up the learning curve faster than you can say "wave-nut"

ⓘFlexibile lesson time- solo or with your scurry

Whether you are a early riser or a sunset shedder, we have got flexibile lessons to match your tree-hopping schedule. Join a group class for some tail-slapping fun with fellow waveRiders or go for a private esson if you prefer quiter beach and full focus on your form.
